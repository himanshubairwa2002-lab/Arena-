# India Playbook — ₹ Economics, Checkout, Funnel, Compliance
*Companion to `IDEAS_DEEP_DIVE.md`. Everything here is the India-first execution layer: what ads cost in ₹, what price converts, how money lands in your account, and what will get you restricted.*

---

## 1. The India ad economics (2026)

| Metric | India (typical) | India range | Compare |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPM — prospecting | **₹80–₹180** | ₹30–₹400 | US ~9× higher ($8.50–$12 ≈ ₹750–₹1,050) |
| CPM — Instagram Reels | **₹30–₹80** (cheapest placement) | ₹45–₹140 | Feed runs 2–3× Reels |
| CPM — retargeting | ₹250–₹500 | up to ₹800 (cart abandoners) | Smaller pools cost more |
| CPC (website click) | **₹3–₹15** | ₹0.50–₹55 by category | EdTech ₹14–₹38; F&B ₹6–₹18; fintech ₹20–₹55 |
| Cost per purchase (D2C) | **₹380–₹950** | ₹380–₹1,200 | e-com benchmark |
| Video view (1,000 views) | ₹25–₹180 | — | Reels is the default format for a reason |
| Practical daily test budget | **₹500–₹1,500/day** | minimum ₹100 | Below ₹500/day, the algorithm rarely exits learning |
| Monthly floor to gather real data | ₹15,000–₹45,000 | ₹15k–₹1.5L | Plan for learning-phase costs, not just clicks |

**Cheapest categories to advertise into:** F&B/quick-commerce (₹90–₹220 CPM, ₹6–₹18 CPC), local services, fitness/wellness (₹100–₹300 CPL), apparel.
**Most expensive:** fintech/BFSI (₹200–₹400 CPM), EdTech with parent targeting (₹160–₹340 CPM).

**The single most useful fact on this page:** the spread between a bad and a good creative in India is roughly **₹30 to ₹400 CPM — 13×**. Creative quality is not a "nice to have" here; it is the entire unit economics. In the US a mediocre ad still works because AOV is high. In India a mediocre ad at ₹30–₹100 AOV simply cannot pay for itself.

## 2. The ₹ maths that decides whether your product is viable

Formula: `CPC = CPM ÷ (CTR × 10)` → `CPA = CPC ÷ conversion rate` → `profit per order = AOV − CPA − fees`

### Scenario A — ₹199 product, average creative
CPM ₹120, CTR 1.2% → CPC ₹10. LP conversion 2% → **CPA ₹500.**
AOV with a ₹99 bump at 30% take = ₹229. **Loss: ₹271 per sale.** Not a business.

### Scenario B — ₹199 product, strong creative
CPM ₹120, CTR 2.5% → CPC ₹4.80. LP conversion 3% → **CPA ₹160.**
AOV ₹229 → **profit ₹69 per order** before fees (~₹5). Thin, but it works at volume, and every buyer is email/WhatsApp-list inventory for the ₹999 upsell.

### Scenario C — ₹499 front-end (the safer design)
CPM ₹120, CTR 2% → CPC ₹6. LP conversion 2.5% → **CPA ₹240.**
AOV = ₹499 + ₹299 bump at 25% (₹75) = **₹574** → **profit ₹334 per order.**
Even at a weak CPA of ₹400, you still clear ₹174.

### The three rules that fall out of this
1. **In India, design for a ₹399–₹699 front end, not ₹99.** Below ₹399 you are betting everything on an exceptional creative.
2. **Always install a bump at 20–30% of front-end price.** It is the cheapest AOV you will ever add (25–40% take rates are normal).
3. **The back end is the business.** A ₹999 bundle, ₹1,499/year membership or ₹4,999 service converts 3–8% of buyers — that's where the profit is, not on the front end.

## 3. India price psychology (what actually converts)

| Price | Buys you | Typical product | Conversion feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| **₹99** | Impulse, low-trust, high volume | one-liner packs, single cheque sheet | converts instantly, near-zero support, bad for ads |
| **₹199–₹299** | The Indian digital sweet spot | planner, recipe pack, template pack, notes bundle | best balance of volume and margin |
| **₹399–₹699** | Serious purchase, needs proof | full bundle, video invite, full-subject pack, tracker system | needs a demo or testimonial in the ad |
| **₹999–₹1,999** | Considered purchase | complete kit, membership, mini-course, agency OS | needs a story, proof, or a person |
| **₹2,499–₹4,999** | Trust purchase | done-with-you, cohort, service | video sales letter / DM conversation closes it |
| **₹4,999+** | Service, not product | 1:1, agency retainer, premium cohort | sales call |

Two Indian quirks worth knowing: **₹499 outsells ₹399 regularly** (round numbers read as "real product" while ₹399 reads as a discount attempt), and **"₹1,999 → ₹499" anchoring works far better in India than percentage-off** — show the original price crossed out.

## 4. Payment & checkout stack (this is where most Indian sellers lose money)

### What to use
| Tool | Fees | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Razorpay** | 2% + 18% GST on the fee (≈2.36% effective) · **UPI currently 0% TDR** | Primary gateway for a real store | KYC needed (PAN, bank, GST if applicable); new-merchant 0% platform fee offer in 2026 with a one-time ₹199 KYC charge |
| **Instamojo** | ~3% domestic; 3% + ₹3 international | Simple digital-file selling, links in bio | Long-standing for PDF downloads; collects via WhatsApp/Instagram links |
| **Topmate / Cosmofeed** | ~5–10% typical | Fastest zero-setup Indian storefront | UPI in 10 minutes, instant PDF delivery; fees hurt at volume |
| **Playto / Ownstreet / Graphy** | 0–10% depending on plan | Creator storefront with INR pricing | Good early-stage when you don't want fixed monthly costs |
| **Shopify + Razorpay** | Shopify subscription + 2% | Order bumps, upsells, real funnels | Best once you're past ₹50k/month and need bumps |

### What NOT to do
- **Don't send Indian buyers to Gumroad or a card-only Western checkout.** Card penetration and cross-border friction destroy conversion; you'll lose 60–80% of intent at the payment step.
- **Don't price in USD for an Indian audience.** ₹ pricing with UPI converts dramatically better.
- **Don't rely on Instagram's own checkout.** Instagram doesn't process the payment; your link does.

### Delivery mechanics
Google Drive link (view-only, reissued monthly) · email with the file attached for small PDFs · Telegram channel for buyers (updates + community, and resistant to the "forward the PDF" problem) · WhatsApp broadcast list for launches (consent-based) · Notion duplicate link for systems.

**Piracy reality:** any PDF you sell in India will be forwarded. Plan for it: sell class-wise packs rather than one 500-page mega file, put the value in *updates, mock tests, video walkthroughs and community* — things that don't survive forwarding — and reissue delivery links.

## 5. GST & compliance basics

- **GST rate on digital products:** 18% (standard slab for digital services).
- **Registration threshold:** generally ₹20 lakh aggregate turnover in most states, ₹10 lakh in special-category states (North-East, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand). Some advisory sources argue there is effectively **no threshold for digital goods/online services**, so register earlier if in doubt.
- **Export of services** (foreign customers paying you) is generally **zero-rated**, which is a genuine advantage of doing both India and foreign sales — but it comes with filing and LUT requirements.
- **Platform fees are separate:** Razorpay charges 18% GST *on its fee*, not on your sale. Nobody collects your GST liability for you — invoices and returns are your job.

**This is genuinely the one place where being cheap will cost you more than an accountant will.** Budget ₹5,000–₹15,000 for a CA to set up GST + invoice format + returns for a digital-product business, and re-read the rules the month you cross ₹20 lakh. Do not copy tax advice from a creator reel.

## 6. The funnel that works in India (Instagram → WhatsApp → UPI)

```
Reel (Hinglish hook, 12–18s)
   ↓
"Comment WORD" (ManyChat/automation DM) — highest-converting CTA in India
   ↓
DM with the link  →  landing page (₹ price, UPI checkout, 1-screen story)
   ↓
Payment (Razorpay / Topmate / Instamojo)
   ↓
Instant delivery (Drive/Telegram) + WhatsApp confirmation
   ↓
WhatsApp community or Telegram channel (retention + updates)
   ↓
₹999 bundle / ₹1,499 membership / service upsell (this is the profit)
```

**Why "comment WORD" beats "link in bio" in India:** DM automation starts a conversation, which is how Indians buy — and it survives the link-in-bio blindness that has set in on Instagram. It also builds a contactable list, which is the only asset you own if your account gets restricted.

**Leaks to plug:** no follow-up sequence after delivery (biggest one) · no ask for a testimonial while the buyer is happy · no price shown until the DM (kills intent) · a file that can't be opened on a phone (60%+ of Indian buyers are mobile-only — test every PDF on a phone).

## 7. Language strategy (it changes everything about price and volume)

| Audience | Language | Price band it supports | Where they live |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Metro Tier-1** | English / light Hinglish | ₹499–₹2,999 | Instagram, Roposo-free, Etsy, LinkedIn |
| **Tier-2 cities** | Hinglish (Roman-script Hindi mixed with English) | ₹299–₹999 | Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube |
| **Tier-3 / small towns** | Pure Hindi / state language | ₹99–₹499 | Instagram Reels, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp |

Two strategic notes: **the volume is in Tier-2/3 but the price ceiling is in Tier-1** — most successful Indian sellers run one product at two price points and two languages, or start Tier-2 (Hindi + ₹299) to build volume, then upsell the Tier-1 audience on the same catalogue. And **regional languages are the most under-served lane in every category in this document** — Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati and Rajasthani exam/craft/recipe content is where competition drops fastest.

## 8. Timing calendar (when to launch what, and when ads are expensive)

| Window | What to sell | Ad cost signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Jan–Mar** | Planners, exam prep (board season), resume/job kits, "new year" journals | **Cheapest CPMs of the year** (Jan 2026 hit a 13-month low in global data; the same pattern holds in India) |
| **Apr–Jun** | Kids' holiday packs, summer camp worksheets, admission prep, wedding season starts | Moderate |
| **Jul–Sep** | Exam prep (state exams), Raksha Bandhan printables, school re-opening bundles | Cheap; good testing window |
| **Oct–Nov** | **Festival economy:** Diwali decor/gifting, Navratri, corporate gifting kits, festival templates | **CPM +30–50% in India; festive season is the worst time to test, best time to sell an existing winner** |
| **Nov–Feb** | **Wedding season:** invitation suites, decor kits, planner kits | High CPM, high intent, highest AOV |
| **Dec–Jan** | Reading trackers, planners, 2027 editions, "new year new me" | Q4 global CPMs +20–40%; India moderate |

**Rule:** test new offers in Jan–Mar and Jul–Sep. Scale proven offers in Oct–Feb. Never launch something unproven in the fortnight before Diwali.

## 9. What realistic Indian earnings look like

From Indian digital-product market analysis (not a promise — a distribution):

| Stage | Monthly revenue | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Month 0–1 (validation) | **₹0–₹5,000** | One live product, bio link, 5–20 warm DMs/posts — most people quit here |
| Month 2–3 (first rhythm) | **₹5,000–₹25,000** | A clear offer, weekly promotion, one proof post |
| Month 4–6 (working side income) | **₹25,000–₹75,000** | Email/WhatsApp list, 2–3 SKUs, repeat promos |
| Month 7–12 (serious side business) | **₹75,000–₹3,00,000+** | Paid ads that work, bundles, renewals, or a strong niche audience |
| Outlier (not the median) | ₹5 lakh+/month | Hit product + large audience or scaled paid acquisition |

The honest read: **the ₹0–₹5,000 first month is the norm, not a failure**, and the jump from ₹25k to ₹75k almost always comes from adding a list and a second/third SKU — not from a better first product.

## 10. Policy & legal traps specific to India

| Trap | Why it bites in India | Safe practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Health claims** | "PCOS khatam", "thyroid theek", "sugar control" — natural copy in India, rejected by Meta and risky under consumer-protection rules | Food-first, tracker-first framing; 18+ targeting; visible "not medical advice"; partner with a registered dietitian/doctor and credit them |
| **Financial claims** | "Guaranteed returns", SIP/stock tips, "double your money" | Sell trackers and systems, never predictions; never show fabricated P&L screenshots |
| **Exam copyright** | Republishing coaching notes, NCERT scans or paid test series is the fastest way to get banned and sued | Original content only; your own mock questions; cite sources |
| **Employment category** | Resume/job content can be pulled into Meta's Employment Special Ad Category | Product framing, no job listings, no salary promises |
| **Income claims (make-money niches)** | "₹1 lakh/month from home" attracts rejections *and* consumer complaints | Teach a skill with real proof; state that results depend on effort |
| **Astrology/predictive claims** | Popular in India, risky in ad policy and consumer law | Frame as cultural/reflective; never predict outcomes |
| **Personal attributes** | "Aapko anxiety hai?" / "Broke ho?" — second-person sensitive framing is now caught even when implied | Third-person product framing: "a tracker for X", not "you have X" |
| **Missing disclaimers** | Affiliate/influencer disclosure and ad-claim substantiation rules apply to creators too | Disclose paid partnerships; keep proof of any claim you make in the ad |

## 11. The 7-day India validation test (≈₹3,500–₹5,000 total)

**Day 0 — pick two ideas from the India top 10**, ideally from different categories (e.g. wedding invites + exam notes). Build one honest, good ₹299–₹499 product each, with a ₹149–₹299 bump. Total build time: one weekend each.

**Days 1–7 — ₹500/day per idea** (₹250 × 2 ad sets if you want to be careful). Reels only. Broad targeting, 18–45, English + Hindi creative variants, "comment WORD" CTA.

### Read the results with these India thresholds

| Signal | Healthy (India) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | **> 1.5%** | Below 1% → the first 2 seconds are dead; refilm the hook, don't touch targeting |
| CPC | **< ₹8** | ₹15+ → creative/audience mismatch |
| Landing-page conversion | **> 2%** | < 1% → the *offer* is wrong (price, promise, or proof) |
| Cost per purchase | **< ₹400** | > ₹700 → kill or move to the bundle as the primary offer |
| Cost per purchase | **< ₹200** | Scale 20–30%/day immediately and add 2 more creatives |
| Comment-to-DM rate | **> 3% of viewers** | Below 1% → the CTA word isn't obvious enough on screen |

**Decision rule after ₹3,500–₹5,000:** you are not looking for profit. You are looking for **one creative with CTR > 1.5% and a cost per purchase under ₹400**. If you find it, you have a business and the next step is a ₹15,000–₹30,000 month. If you find it in neither idea, change the *offer*, not the ad — this is where most people burn money repeating a creative that was never the problem.

## 12. The one-page summary

1. **Price at ₹399–₹699, not ₹99**, unless your creative is exceptional — India's CPM spread is 13×, and cheap traffic is a myth.
2. **Install a bump on day one**; it's the cheapest AOV you'll ever add.
3. **Sell updates and community, not static files** — forwards are inevitable.
4. **Razorpay/Topmate + UPI, priced in ₹** — never send Indian buyers to a Western card checkout.
5. **"Comment WORD" beats "link in bio"** — build the contactable list.
6. **Test Jan–Mar and Jul–Sep; scale Oct–Feb.** Never launch blind before Diwali.
7. **Photograph your own work, not your dreams** — the ₹0–₹5,000 first month is normal; the people who get to ₹75k added a list and a second SKU.
