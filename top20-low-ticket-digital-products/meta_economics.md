## 1. The 2026 cost reality (US benchmarks)

| Metric | 2026 typical | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | **$8.50–$12.00** | Q4 pushes 20–40% higher; Q1 is cheapest (Jan 2026 hit a 13-month low around **$15.74** in one dataset, vs a Nov peak of ~$25.22 elsewhere) |
| CPC (link clicks) | **$0.70–$1.30** | Reels tends to be cheaper than Feed on the Facebook side |
| Cost per purchase | **$25–$70** | e-commerce / mid-AOV offers |
| Conversion rate (e-com median) | **~1.5%** | Down ~4.7% YoY — the click got cheaper, the conversion got harder |
| Median ROAS | **1.88–1.93×** | Account averages run ~2.98×; 3–4× is the "healthy DTC" target |
| CTR (median, feed) | **~2.39%** | Up ~16% YoY — creative quality is doing more of the work now |
| Instagram share of time watching Reels | **~50%** | Reels is the primary engagement surface on both Facebook and Instagram |

**Cheapest-to-acquire verticals:** e-learning (~$26.80 CPA), baby (~$29.61), books & music (~$30.69), education (~$7.85 CPA, 13.58% CVR), fitness (14.29% CVR).

### What this means for a $9 product

> $9 product, $30 cost per purchase → **you lose $21 per sale, forever.**
> $25 AOV, $30 CPA → still losing.
> $25 AOV, $30 CPA, but 6% of buyers take a $99 back-end within 30 days → **profitable at roughly 1.2× — and that is the whole game.**

Low ticket is a **customer acquisition strategy**, not a business model. Plan the ladder before you plan the product.

## 2. The ladder that makes low ticket work

| Layer | Price | Target take rate | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front-end | $9–$17 | — | Gets the sale on cold traffic |
| Order bump | $9–$19 | 25–40% | Covers the ad bill |
| Upsell | $29–$47 | 10–20% | Pushes campaign into profit |
| Back-end | $11–$29/mo or $59–$199/yr | 3–8% | Turns traffic into a business |
| **Resulting AOV** | | | **~$25–$45** |

**Worked example (rank #1 idea — budget systems):**
- Front end: $9 paycheck-plan printable — 100 sales → $900
- Bump: $19 cash-stuffing binder kit, 30% take → 30 sales → $570
- Upsell: $37 debt-payoff vault, 15% take → 15 sales → $555
- Revenue $2,025 · Ad spend at $30 CPA × 100 = $3,000 → **still negative**, which is why the email sequence that converts 5–8% into the $9/mo printable club (or $59/yr) is not optional.

**Rule of thumb: your front-end price should be ≤ 1/3 of your target cost-per-purchase.** If your CPA is $30, you need a $9–$12 tripwire *with* bumps — or you need a $27–$47 bundle as the primary offer.

## 3. The 7-day, ~$150 validation protocol

**Day 1 — pick 2 ideas from the top 10, not 1.** Choose one from different categories (e.g. budget systems + ADHD planner). Build:
- a one-page product (Canva → PDF or Google Sheet), 20–40 pages, genuinely useful
- a 1-page sales page (Gumroad / Payhip / Stan / Shopify + Stripe handles this in an hour)
- a $9–$12 price with a $19 bump already in the checkout

**Day 2 — write 3 creatives, not 3 ads.** Same offer, three different hooks:
1. **Problem callout** — "Your paycheck disappears in 3 days."
2. **Demo** — screen-record or hands-only flip-through, 12 seconds, no intro
3. **Social proof / count** — "1,400 people used this last month."

Under 15 seconds, text hook in the first 0.5s, no logo, no talking-head intro. Reels-native.

**Days 3–7 — spend $20–$25/day.**
- 1 campaign, 2 ad sets, broad targeting (18–65+, no interest stacking)
- Optimise for **Purchase** if you have any pixel data at all; otherwise start on the cheapest conversion event you can get volume for
- All 3 creatives inside one ad set (let the algorithm pick)

### Kill / scale thresholds

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | < 0.8% after 3,000 impressions | Hook is dead → new first 2 seconds |
| CPC | > $2.00 | Creative/audience mismatch → rewrite, don't re-target |
| Landing page CVR | < 1% after 200 clicks | **Offer** problem, not ad problem → fix price, promise or page |
| Cost per purchase | > 2× your AOV | Not viable cold → cut, or test the bundle as the primary offer |
| Cost per purchase | < 1/3 of AOV | **Scale 20–30%/day**, add the next 2 creative variations |
| ROAS | ≥ 1.5 on front end alone | Profitable already — everything after this is back end |

**Decision rule after 7 days:** you're not looking for profit at $150. You're looking for *one* creative with a CTR above 1.2% and a cost-per-purchase inside 1.5× AOV. That means the offer is real. Everything else is maths and volume.

## 4. Ad-policy traps that will get your account restricted

The categories ranked high here mostly avoid these — the ones ranked lower don't. Read before spending.

| Trap | What trips it | Safe alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal attributes** | Any copy implying you know the viewer's health, finances, mental state, or faith — including indirect forms: "for people managing blood sugar", "are you stuck in a scarcity mindset?", "tired of your belly fat?" | Talk about the product and the routine, in third person. "A 30-day tracker for appointments and symptoms." |
| **Health & wellness claims** | Specific outcomes, timelines, cure/prevention claims, before-and-after framing around weight or the body, blood-sugar claims. Weight-management ads require 18+ targeting and stricter classification | Food-first, tool-first framing: "50 high-protein recipes". Never "lose X in Y". Note: Meta rewrote its health & wellness policy in July 2026 (before/after allowed for cosmetic, 18+), and separately tightened implied-transformation enforcement — the direction of travel is *more* restriction, not less |
| **Money / income claims** | "Make $5k/month", guaranteed earnings, screenshots of dashboards as proof | Sell the skill and the system; show the work, not the payout |
| **Employment Special Ad Category (US)** | Résumé/job-search creative that reads as promoting *job opportunities* | Product framing, no job listings, no "apply now", exclude under-18s |
| **Copyright & trademarks** | Canva/Instagram/Notion logos or UI in creative; ©-ed assets inside templates you resell; exam boards' proprietary question banks; celebrity or franchise likeness in personalised products | Your own UI mockups; original content; licensed or self-made assets only |
| **MRR/PLR resale** | Selling "resell rights" to content you don't own, or that contradicts the licence you bought | Only resell where the licence explicitly permits it, and say plainly what the buyer is allowed to do |

**Practical safety net:** put a qualified disclaimer near any health or finance claim, keep your landing page honest about what's inside, and run ads from a Business Manager with a verified domain and clean history — one restriction is survivable, three is a pattern.

## 5. Build-and-deliver stack (all under ~$50 to start)

| Need | Fastest option |
|---|---|
| Product build | Canva (PDFs/planners), Google Sheets (budget systems), Notion (business OS), CapCut (audio/video products) |
| Checkout + delivery | Gumroad / Payhip (fastest), Stan Store (best for IG bios), Whop (courses, communities, memberships), Shopify + Stripe (most control, best for bumps/upsells) |
| Order bump / upsell | Built into Shopify, Whop and Stan; on Gumroad use a bundle on the receipt page |
| Email (this is the actual business) | Kit / MailerLite / Beehiiv — a 5-email post-purchase sequence takes an afternoon and doubles effective AOV |
| Creative | CapCut, phone camera, Screen Studio or Loom for demos, CapCut auto-captions |
| Tracking | Meta pixel + Conversions API (Shopify/Gumroad apps handle it), one looker-upper for UTM sanity |

## 6. What to do on Monday

1. Open `dashboard.html` and sort by **"fastest to launch"** — pick two ideas from the top 10 that you could honestly build in a weekend.
2. Build one of them properly to a $9–$12 price with a $19 bump. Make it *genuinely* good; refunds kill low-ticket offers faster than bad ads do.
3. Write 3 hooks. Record 3 Reels. Post them organically **first** — 48 hours of organic data tells you which hook is worth paying for.
4. Only then put $20/day behind the best one, and read §3 again before you judge the result.

**The honest summary:** the ideas at the top of this list are unglamorous, they are crowded, and they work — because the buyer's pain is immediate, the price is trivial, and the offer can be stacked into a $30+ order. The ideas at the bottom are the ones people make YouTube videos about instead of building.
