## PART A — The ₹99 One-Liners Pack: complete structure

**Cover:** `CET Senior Secondary — राजस्थान GK 500 One-Liners | हिंदी | Edition: Nov 2026 | [Your Brand]`

**Specs:** A4 · 5 columns · 22 pages · B&W print-friendly · ~23 facts/page · 12–13 pt Hindi font · every file under 20 MB

| 10 | **प्रतीक एवं विविध** | 20 | 1 | 2–3 | yearly |
| | **TOTAL** | **500** | **22** | | |

**Why Art & Culture gets the biggest slice (90 facts, 18%):** it is the highest-yield, hardest-to-fake section of Rajasthan GK, it repeats every cycle, and national sellers consistently get it wrong. It is your moat.

### Content rules (non-negotiable)

- **One fact per line.** No sub-clauses, no sentences. Maximum 9 words.
- **Bold the keyword** (चिंकरा, गोडावण, कालीबंगा) — the eye scans for the bold.
- **Section header every 8–10 lines**; page number + section name in every footer.
- **No colour. No borders.** ₹1–2/page at any local print shop.
- **Footer on every page:** `सुधार/सुझाव: [WhatsApp number]` — this turns your biggest risk (accuracy) into a trust signal.
- **Edition date on the cover**, plus a **revision-log page** listing what changed. That log is what makes the monthly pack worth paying for again.

## PART B — ⚠️ Volatile facts: verify or leave out

Never print these without checking the official source the week you publish.

| Fact type | Why it is dangerous | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **District count & boundaries** | Reorganised in 2023 and revised again — old books and old PDFs are wrong | Never print a total without checking rajasthan.gov.in |
| **Current CM / Governor / Ministers / Speaker** | Changes; date-stamps instantly | Print "as of <month year>" |
| **Scheme names & amounts** | Names and numbers change every budget | Print "as of <month year>" |
| **Budget figures, GSDP, per-capita** | Annual | Cite the year |
| **Exam pattern, marks, negative marking, shifts** | Changes per notification | Read **this cycle's official notification only** |
| **Census / literacy / sex-ratio stats** | Cite the census year, never "current" | Always date it |
| **"Largest / tallest / first" records** | Get superseded | Verify or drop |

**Standard footer line for every volatile page:**

> `आँकड़े [माह वर्ष] तक अद्यतन। ताज़ा जानकारी के लिए RSSB की आधिकारिक वेबसाइट देखें।`
