## 2. The market, described properly

### Who the buyer actually is

Not "students". Four distinct buyers, with different wallets:

| Buyer | Age | What they feel | What they can pay | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The repeat aspirant** | 23–30 | "Third attempt. Coaching is ₹40,000. I need material I can actually revise from." | ₹299–₹1,499 | Telegram, YouTube, coaching WhatsApp groups |
| **The first-timer** | 19–23 | "Where do I even start? What's the syllabus? What's important?" | ₹99–₹499 | Instagram Reels, YouTube, college groups |
| **The working aspirant** | 25–34 | "I have 2 hours a day, max. Give me the compressed version." | ₹499–₹1,499 | Telegram during office hours, WhatsApp at night |
| **The parent / coaching institute** | 35–55 | "My child needs it" / "My students need material to print." | ₹1,499–₹5,000 | Face-to-face, phone, local reputation |

**The fourth buyer is the one everyone ignores**, and it's the fastest money: one coaching institute in Jaipur, Kota, Sikar, Alwar or Bhilwara buying a licence for 100 students at ₹3,000 is 30 consumer sales in a single conversation.

### How many of them are there

- Rajasthan's government-job pipeline is one of the largest in India — REET Mains alone ran over four days in January 2026 with thousands of teacher vacancies on offer, and RSSB's 2026 calendar spans **five recruitment exams from 17 November 2026 to 5 February 2027**.
- The Telegram ecosystem around Indian exam prep is enormous and measurable: a single UPSC channel directory lists **765+ channels**, with individual channels running **90,000–600,000 subscribers**. Rajasthan-specific CET/Patwar/REET groups are a fraction of that in scale but far less served.
- Compare to what they already spend: exam fees, ₹1,000–₹40,000 coaching, ₹300–₹800 on printed test series per month. **A ₹299 PDF is the cheapest line item in their entire preparation.**

### What they already buy (so you're competing with reality, not imagination)

| What exists | Price | Its weakness (your opening) |
|---|---|---|
| Free Telegram PDFs | ₹0 | Scanned, undated, unorganised, often pirated coaching material. Unusable on a phone. |
| National apps (Testbook, Adda247, Unacademy) | ₹1,000–₹5,000/yr | Strong content, but generic. Rajasthan-specific GK is a thin corner of a big app, and it's not in the format people revise from. |
| Local coaching printed notes | ₹500–₹3,000 (offline) | Good content, bad access. You must travel, buy photocopies, no updates. |
| Coaching institute test series | ₹1,000–₹4,000 | Excellent, but only available if enrolled. |
| **You** | **₹99–₹499** | **Narrow, current, print-ready, Hindi, phone-readable, updated monthly, available tonight.** |

### The honest competitive picture

**The competition is severe, and it's free.** There is no pretending otherwise: Telegram is full of channels giving away Rajasthan GK PDFs to 50,000+ subscribers.

So the business is **not selling information.** Information is worthless here. The business is selling:

1. **Organisation** — "syllabus-mapped, topic-wise, dated, and in the order you'll actually be tested."
2. **Currency** — "updated after the notification, and refreshed monthly for current affairs."
3. **Format** — "readable on a phone, printable on A4, with an answer key you can trust."
4. **Pattern intelligence** — "which topics gave how many questions in the last six exams." Nobody free does this.
5. **Accountability** — a WhatsApp group with a 45-day plan and a weekly check-in.

If you build a generic "Rajasthan GK PDF", you will make ₹0. If you build the above, ₹499 is easy to charge.

## 3. The exact product — module by module

### Pick ONE exam. This is non-negotiable.

Build for **CET Senior Secondary** first (exam 1–3 Dec 2026). It has the largest, most anxious audience, the shortest syllabus, and the closest deadline. Everything else is a clone of this build.

### The v1 product: "CET Senior Secondary — Final 45 Day Revision Pack" (Hindi)

| 4 | **"Kya aata hai" — 6-year question analysis** | 15–20 | Topic-wise frequency tables for the last 6 exam cycles: how many questions each topic produced, which sub-topics repeat, and a "leave this" list of low-yield topics | ₹299 |

**Total build: ~230 pages across all files.** Realistically **10–14 evenings** if you know the material, or 10–14 evenings plus ₹3,000–₹5,000 paid to a B.Ed/university student for a first draft if you don't.

### Sample content — the standard you're aiming for

**One-liners (format example — verify every fact yourself before selling):**

> राजस्थान का राज्य पशु — चिंकरा
> राजस्थान का राज्य पक्षी — गोडावण (ग्रेट इंडियन बस्टर्ड)
> राजस्थान का राज्य वृक्ष — खेजड़ी
> राजस्थान का राज्य पुष्प — रोहिड़ा
> राजस्थान का सर्वोच्च शिखर — गुरु शिखर (माउंट आबू)
> कालीबंगा — हनुमानगढ़ जिला, सिंधु घाटी सभ्यता
> सांभर झील — देश की सबसे बड़ी अंतर्देशीय खारी झील, रामसर साइट
> जयपुर (गुलाबी नगर) की स्थापना — 1727, सवाई जयसिंह द्वितीय
> राजस्थान का सबसे बड़ा जिला (क्षेत्रफल) — जैसलमेर
> हवा महल — जयपुर, 1799

**Two things to notice, and both matter:**
- **The format is the product.** No colour, no borders, five columns, 12–13 pt Hindi font, printable on a ₹1,500 local printer. That is what makes it usable and what makes it different from the free scans.
- **⚠️ District counts, division counts, scheme names and "current" holders of posts change constantly.** Rajasthan's district structure has been reorganised more than once in recent years. Every single factual line must be checked against the **latest RSSB/REET syllabus, official state sources and a recent standard reference**, and re-checked each exam cycle. Put a "corrections & updates" line with your WhatsApp number in the footer — it turns a dangerous weakness into a trust signal.

**Reel hook (Hindi, 12 seconds, faceless, text-on-screen + screen recording):**
> *"CET Sen Sec mein Rajasthan GK se 40+ questions aate hain. Ye 6 saal ka data hai — dekho kaunse topic sabse zyada aate hain."*
> (Screen recording: your analysis table, scrolling.)

### What NOT to put in the pack (these lose you money)

- ❌ Endless theory and long essays — nobody revises from them
- ❌ Copied coaching material, NCERT scans, or paid test-series questions (**this ends your business**)
- ❌ Anything you can't source — one wrong fact in a viral screenshot is a week of damage control
- ❌ 300 MB single files — split by subject, keep every file under 20 MB
- ❌ Designs that need colour printing — parents print black-and-white at ₹1–2/page

## 4. The money maths

### The ladder

| Layer | Price | Take rate | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | 30–50% of viewers | 10-page sample (captures WhatsApp number) |
| Front end | **₹99** | high, impulse | 500 one-liners |
| Bump | **+₹199** | 30–40% | core notes + 6-year analysis |
| Upsell | **₹499** | 15–25% | everything + 3 mocks + 45-day plan |
| Recurring | **₹199/mo or ₹1,499/yr** | 5–8% of buyers | monthly current affairs + new mocks + doubt group |
| B2B | **₹2,000–₹5,000** | a handful | coaching/tuition bulk licence |

**Expected average order value: ₹280–₹420** depending on how well the bump and upsell are presented.

### Organic first (this is the important part)

Your first 30–100 sales should cost **₹0 in ads**. Telegram and WhatsApp groups are free distribution, and the free sample is free to deliver.

| Scenario | Buyers | AOV | Revenue | Cost | **Net** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Weak month** | 40 | ₹280 | ₹11,200 | ₹500 (tools) | **₹10,700** |
| **Realistic month 1** | 80 | ₹320 | ₹25,600 | ₹1,000 | **₹24,600** |
| **Good month 1** (2 bulk licences) | 90 + 2 bulk | ₹380 + ₹6,000 | ₹40,200 | ₹2,000 | **₹38,200** |
| **If you add ads** | 43 sales at ₹350 CPA | ₹320 | ₹13,760 | ₹15,000 | **–₹1,240 ❌** |

**Read that last row carefully.** At a ₹350 cost per purchase (realistic for EdTech in India — the vertical runs ₹160–₹340 CPM with ₹200–₹600 cost per lead), a ₹99 front end **cannot** pay for ads. This is the single most important thing to understand about this idea:

> **Ads only work if you lead with the ₹299 pack, not the ₹99 one-liners.**
> Lead with ₹299 in paid traffic (CPA ₹350 → still thin, but survivable with the bump), and use ₹99 as the *downsell* for people who don't convert — or sell ₹99 purely organically, where acquisition is free.

### Break-even and the real money

- **Break-even on ads:** you need AOV ≈ 1.2 × CPA, i.e. **AOV ₹420+ against a ₹350 CPA**. That means the bump must be working (30%+) before you scale spend.
- **Where the money actually is:** a yearly pack at ₹1,499 sold 40 times is **₹59,960** — more than 200 sales of the ₹99 pack. And it renews.
- **The compounding asset:** every free-sample download gives you a WhatsApp number. 500 numbers, posted to twice a month, is a business that survives a bad ad month, an account restriction, or a competitor copying you.

## 5. The customer's whole journey

```
SEE      → Reel in Hindi: "CET mein Rajasthan GK se 40+ questions"  (screen-recording + text)
          ↓
ACT      → Comment "SAMPLE" → automated DM (ManyChat/InstaDM) asks for WhatsApp number
          ↓
RECEIVE  → 10-page sample arrives on WhatsApp within 60 seconds. Your name + number on every page.
          ↓
SELL     → Day 0: "Poora pack ₹99 ka hai — 500 one-liners. Notes ke saath ₹299. Chahiye?"
          Day 2: "Aapne sample padha? Mock test baaki hai — ₹499 mein 3 full mocks + 45-din plan."
          Day 5: "Exam 25 din door hai. Yearly pack ₹1,499 — mahine ke naye current affairs + doubt group."
          Day 10: last nudge: "Sirf 18 din bache. Aaj join karo."
          ↓
PAY      → UPI (Razorpay/Topmate link) — ₹99/₹299/₹499, prices visible
          ↓
GET      → Google Drive link + WhatsApp confirmation, delivered in under 5 minutes
          ↓
STAY     → Added to the exam cohort WhatsApp group. Gets the monthly current-affairs sheet.
          ↓
RENEW    → Next exam notification → yearly pack → and in 2 years, your ₹4,999 crash course
```

**Four moments where money is made or lost:**
1. **The comment → WhatsApp step.** If you don't collect the number, you lose 90% of the audience forever. Never just say "link in bio".
2. **The first 10 minutes after delivery.** Ask one question: *"Kaunsa subject sabse weak lag raha hai?"* A reply turns a buyer into a relationship, and the relationship buys the ₹1,499 pack.
3. **The 48 hours after the free sample.** This is your highest-intent window, and it closes fast. Three follow-ups, not one.
4. **The notification day.** The moment a new RSSB notification drops, everyone in your list is suddenly in buying mode. Be ready with the next exam's pack that day.

## 6. First sales: where and what to say

### Channel 1 — Telegram (your fastest free distribution)

Search these terms and join 15–20 groups: `CET`, `Rajasthan CET`, `Patwar`, `REET`, `Rajasthan GK`, `RSSB`, `Rajasthan Exam`.

**Rules of engagement:** read each group's rules first. Many allow file sharing, many don't. Where it's allowed, post the *free sample*, never a sales pitch:

> "CET Sen Sec ke liye Rajasthan GK ke 10-page one-liners banaye hain (free). Kaam ke lagte hain toh batao, bhej deta hoon."

Where it's not allowed, **DM the admins** — they're often running a channel with 20,000+ subscribers and are happy to promote a genuinely useful free resource in exchange for a mention or a cut. One admin relationship = one distribution channel.

### Channel 2 — WhatsApp (where the actual buying happens)

- Every aspirant you know is in 3–5 study groups. Ask two friends to forward the sample.
- Ask your local tuition teacher or library to share it with their students.
- **Create your own group:** "CET Sen Sec 2026 — 45 Day Plan". Post one practice question a day. That group *is* your business, and it's free.

### Channel 3 — Coaching institutes (the fastest money)

Walk in or call 10 institutes/tuition centres in Jaipur and nearby towns (Sikar, Alwar, Bhilwara, Ajmer, Tonk). Owner-to-owner script:

> "Sir/Madam, main Rajesh (your name) — CET Sen Sec ke liye revision pack banaya hai: 500 one-liners, 6 saal ka question analysis, 3 full mock tests with answer key, aur 45-din ka plan. ₹3,000 ka licence, 100 students tak print kar sakte hain, aapke institute ka naam bhi laga sakte hain. Sample bhej doon?"

Why this works: institutes constantly need printed material, they pay without negotiation at this price, and one yes replaces 30 individual sales. **Offer them a branded version** — their logo on the cover. That single feature closes deals.

### Channel 4 — Instagram Reels (free, compounds)

Five formats that work, all faceless:
1. **Screen-recording of your own question analysis** — "dekho, 6 saal ka data kya kehta hai"
2. **One-liner flash** — 10 facts, 10 seconds, fast cuts, "save this"
3. **"Ye galti mat karo"** — a common preparation mistake
4. **Day-in-the-life of the 45-day plan** — "Din 7/45, aaj ka topic"
5. **Mock question of the day** — show a question, answer in the comments, "poora mock pack bio mein"

Comment trigger: **"SAMPLE"** → DM automation → WhatsApp number. That is your whole funnel.

### The DM script (copy this)

> "Hi! Sample WhatsApp pe bhej diya hai ✅
> Poora pack ₹99 ka hai — 500 one-liners (Rajasthan GK, subject-wise).
> Notes + 6 saal ka analysis ke saath ₹299.
> Mocks + 45-din plan ke saath ₹499.
> Aapko kaunsa chahiye? Payment UPI se, PDF turant 👍"

Then, on day 2, without waiting for a reply:

> "Aapne mock test try kiya? 150 questions, 2.5 ghante, negative marking — asli exam jaisa. ₹499 mein 3 mock + answer key + 45-din plan. Ek mock try karke dekho, phir batao."

## 9. Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | How bad | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Copyright** — republishing coaching PDFs, NCERT scans or paid test-series questions | 🔴 Business-ending. Legal exposure, not just a ban. | Write 100% original content. Make your own mock questions. Cite public sources. Never repackage a paid product. |
| **Accuracy** — one wrong answer in a mock | 🟠 Reputation-ending in this community | Have a subject teacher verify. Publish "corrections welcome" with a number. Fix and re-issue the same day. |
| **Piracy on Telegram** | 🟡 Inevitable | Price for volume, and put the value in updates, new mocks and the doubt group — none of which survive forwarding. |
| **Missing the exam window** | 🟠 Costs real money, no recovery | Ship the ₹99 pack within 7 days even if the full pack isn't finished. A late-perfect product earns less than an early-good one. |
| **Exam-body claims** — "selection guaranteed", implying RSSB affiliation | 🔴 Policy + legal | You sell study material. Say so plainly. No result promises, no official logos. |
| **Seasonal dead months** | 🟡 Apr–Jun can be quiet | Use them to build the next exam's pack (CET Graduate for January, Patwar, Forest Guard) and to write the ₹1,499 yearly content. |
| **Refund demands** | 🟢 Low in this category | Free sample removes most of it. Always include the "what's inside" page so nobody buys blind. |

## 10. Faceless production workflow

**You never appear on camera, and you never need to speak.**

| Element | How |
|---|---|
| Reels | Screen-record your own PDFs and tables on a phone; add Hindi text captions; trending audio or no audio |
| Voice | Optional — use your own voice or a Hindi AI voice. Most CET content performs fine with text-on-screen only. |
| Delivery | Google Drive folder + a WhatsApp Business auto-reply template |
| Support | Two fixed windows a day ("7–8 PM aur 9–10 PM jawab milega") — that's the promise and the boundary |
| The group | WhatsApp Community, one group per exam, admin-only posting for announcements |

**Time budget per day once live:** 45 minutes to answer DMs and doubts, 45 minutes to create one Reel and the daily content. That's 1.5 hours/day — a real side business, not a full-time job.

## 11. Your Jaipur advantage

- **You can walk into coaching institutes.** Sikar, Kota, Jaipur, Alwar and Bhilwara are dense with them. A phone call is not as good as standing in the office with a printed sample in your hand.
- **You can verify facts locally** — local libraries, college faculty, senior aspirants, and the state archives are within reach. That's a real moat over national sellers.
- **Rajasthan GK is your home subject.** Art, culture, fairs, dialects, local geography and schemes are exactly what outsiders get wrong and what Rajasthan papers test hardest.
- **You can meet your buyer.** Spend one Sunday at a coaching centre or a library exam hall. You will learn more about what to build in one afternoon than a month of internet research.

# IDEA 2 — THE INDIAN WEDDING KIT

**🇮🇳 India-first (exportable later) · ₹499 → ₹4,999 · Season: mid-November to February, then April to June**

## 1. The idea in one page

| | |

| **What it is** | A bundle of **editable** wedding materials — Canva invitation suites, WhatsApp video invites, mehendi/haldi/sangeet decor printables, day-of signage, checklists and a ₹ budget tracker — delivered instantly as files the buyer edits themselves. |
| **Who buys** | Engaged couples 24–35 (metro + Tier-2), siblings/cousins who run the wedding, families planning a ₹5–₹50 lakh wedding who still won't pay a designer, **and wedding decorators/planners/photographers who use it for client work**. |

## 4. The money maths

### The ladder

| Layer | Price | Take rate | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | 35–50% | 10-page sample (1 sheet per subject) |
| Front end | **₹249** | — | One class, all subjects, ~180 pages, with answer key |
| Bump ⭐ | **+₹199** | 35% | Holiday homework pack (30 dated sheets) |
| Upsell | **₹999** | 15% | One class, **full year** — includes the monthly fresh sheets |

| B2B | **₹4,999** | a handful | Tuition/school licence (print for 30 students) |

**Expected AOV: ₹380–₹480.** The math: ₹249 + (35% × ₹199 = ₹70) + (15% × ₹999 = ₹150) ≈ **₹469**.

| **Organic only, month 1** (2 school groups + IG) | 25 | ₹440 | ₹11,000 | ₹500 | **₹10,500** |

**Break-even on ads:** with a ₹469 AOV, you break even at roughly a **₹390 cost per purchase** — and worksheets sit in friendly, cheap, non-competitive ad categories (education CPM ~₹160–₹340, and parenting content gets organic reach for free). **The engine:** every parent who buys the ₹999 full-year pack gets a monthly email/WhatsApp with new sheets. At month 3, they're still using it. At renewal, 60–70% say yes, because their child has done the sheets every week for a year and the stack of printed pages in the house *is* the proof of value.

**The two moments to engineer deliberately:**

2. **The first WhatsApp message after delivery should ask for a photo of the child's work.** "Bachche ki sheet ki photo bhej dijiye — dekhna hai kaam aaya ya nahi." A photo reply gives you (a) your best testimonial, (b) permission to use it, and (c) a conversation that sells the ₹999 yearly pack.

## 6. First sales: where and what to say

### Channel 1 — School WhatsApp groups ⭐ (this is the whole business)

Every parent group in India tolerates genuinely useful material. The route in:

1. Find a **teacher** or a **parent representative** (a friend, relative or neighbour) in a Class 1–3 group.
2. Give them the free 10-page sample and say: *"Group mein share kar dijiye, free hai. Pasand aaye toh poora pack ₹249 ka hai."*
3. When a parent replies, deliver the sample instantly and follow up the next evening.

**Why this works:** the recommendation comes from a trusted person inside a closed group, not from an ad. It's the cheapest and highest-converting channel in India for this product, and one good group can produce 10–20 sales.

### Channel 2 — Tuition teachers and small coaching centres ⭐ (highest value)

Visit or call 15 tuition teachers and primary-school coaching centres in your area. Pitch:

> "Madam/Sir, Class 1 (ya Class 2) ke liye printable worksheets ka pack banaya hai — maths, English, Hindi, EVS, activity, sab CBSE-aligned, answer key ke saath. Aapke paas 15 bachche hain to ek baar ₹4,999 mein poora saal ka licence le lijiye — 30 students tak print kar sakti hain, aapke institute ka naam bhi laga dunga. Har mahine naye sheets bhi bhejta rahunga."

**Why this closes:** a teacher who currently spends 3 hours a week making or hunting worksheets will pay to get that time back, and the material looks professional in front of parents. One teacher = ₹4,999 and a referral network.

### Channel 3 — Instagram (free, faceless, fast)

Five formats that work for this niche:
1. **The printed stack flip-through** — the "satisfying stack" shot
2. **A real sheet being filled** — hand + pencil + paper, overhead
3. **"Phone vs worksheet" side-by-side** — the parent guilt hook
4. **"Printing cost ₹150" calculator** — kills the last objection
5. **Teacher testimonial** — "3 saal se aise sheets banati thi, ab ready-made mil jaati hain"

Comment trigger: **"SAMPLE"**.

### Channel 4 — Facebook parent groups & Pinterest

- **Facebook:** "mom groups", "parenting India", "homeschool India", city-specific parenting groups. Share the sample as a gift; don't drop links.
- **Pinterest:** pin every worksheet page mockup as its own pin — "Class 1 maths worksheets", "matra worksheets", "nursery admission preparation", "summer holiday activities". This is free traffic that keeps producing for years, and this niche is Pinterest's home turf.

### The DM script (copy this)

> "Aapka bachcha kaunsi class mein hai? 📚
> Main 10-page free sample bhej deta hoon — maths, English, Hindi, EVS ka ek-ek page.
> Poora pack ₹249 ka hai (180 pages, answer key ke saath, CBSE-aligned).
> Print ka kharcha sirf ₹150–₹200 hoga.
> Pasand aaye toh bataiye, UPI se payment, PDF turant 👍"

Asking the class first is deliberate — it tells you which pack to pitch and gives the parent a reason to reply.

## 9. Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | How bad | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Piracy / forwarding** | 🟡 Guaranteed in India | Never sell one giant file. Sell class-wise packs, keep the freshness in the monthly membership, reissue Drive links monthly. Price for volume. |
| **AI-garbled Hindi** | 🟠 Instant credibility death | Proofread every Hindi sheet by hand. Get a teacher's review. Run a final print test. |
| **Wrong answers** | 🟠 Refunds + bad WhatsApp group reputation | Teacher review (₹2,000–₹3,000) is the cheapest insurance you'll buy. Add "found a mistake? WhatsApp us" in the footer. |
| **Seasonality (Apr–Jun, Dec–Jan)** | 🟢 Mild; peaks help you | Build holiday packs ahead of each peak. January–March and April–June are your harvest months. |
| **Free PDF competition** | 🟡 Constant | Compete on organisation, answer keys, Hindi medium and freshness — not on price. Never go below ₹199. |
| **Overpromising academically** ("topper banega") | 🟠 Policy-adjacent + trust | Sell practice, structure and screen-free time. That's what the parent is actually buying. |
| **Children's faces/images in ads** | 🟠 Ethical + legal | Use hands only, or a child's back. Get written parental consent for any photo you use. Best: no children at all, just the printed pages. |

## 11. Your Jaipur advantage

- **Dense school ecosystem.** Jaipur has hundreds of CBSE and ICSE schools plus thousands of tuition teachers and coaching centres within reach. You can physically visit 10 in one day.
- **Hindi-medium demand is local and real.** Most national sellers are English-only; Jaipur/Tier-2 Rajasthan is Hindi-medium heavy — a lane with far less competition and very high need.
- **Price point fits the market.** ₹249–₹1,499 works for Jaipur, Jaipur-rural and nearby towns far better than the ₹3,000+ workbook packs.
- **Teacher access.** A friend, relative or neighbour teaches in a primary school — that's one phone call to a review, a bulk licence and 15 parent referrals.
- **Printing is cheap and local.** You can print 3 physical sample sets for ₹500 and hand them to teachers. Physical samples close deals in this market; a PDF link doesn't.

## Which to build first — the honest answer

**Start with #1 (exam notes), this week.** Not because it's the best business, but because:
- **The window closes on 3 December.** CET Sen Sec is the hardest deadline of the three, and deadlines create sales.
- **It's the cheapest to start** (₹0–₹500) and the fastest to first sale (Telegram + WhatsApp, no ad spend).
