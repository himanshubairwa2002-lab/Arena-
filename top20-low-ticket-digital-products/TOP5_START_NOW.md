# BRIEF 1 — Rajasthan CET / State Exam Notes 🇮🇳

*(This is the timing play. The window is open right now and closes 3 December.)*

## The business in one line
Sell high-yield, exam-specific, **Hindi-language** notes, one-liners and mock tests to Rajasthan government-job aspirants — starting with **CET Senior Secondary (1–3 Dec)**, then **CET Graduate (16–18 Jan)**, then Patwari, Agriculture Supervisor and Forest Guard.

## Why this works, and why it works *right now*

- The RSSB released its revised 2026 calendar on 28 September: **five recruitment exams between 17 Nov 2026 and 5 Feb 2027.** CET Senior Secondary is 1–3 December — that's ~8 weeks away, which is exactly when aspirants start panic-buying revision material.
- The demand is desperate and the price is trivial relative to what they're already spending on forms, travel and coaching. ₹199 for high-yield notes is noise.
- **Your unfair advantage is location.** You are in Rajasthan. Rajasthan GK — history, art & culture, geography, schemes, current affairs — is a subject where local knowledge beats generic national content. Most national sellers write generic "GK" and get Rajasthan wrong.
- The competition is real (Telegram is flooded with free PDFs) but the *free* material is mostly badly formatted, undated, and in unreadable scans. A clean, print-ready, mobile-readable, syllabus-mapped pack wins on presentation alone.

**Money proof:** Telegram exam ecosystems are enormous (a single UPSC channel directory lists 765+ channels, with individual channels at 90k–600k subscribers), national players (Testbook, Adda247) sell ₹1,000–₹5,000/year subscriptions to this exact audience, and Indian digital-product guidance lists study-note PDFs at ₹149–₹399 as a top-selling SKU. India EdTech CPM runs ₹160–₹340 with CPL ₹200–₹600 — high, which is exactly why the first sales should come free.

## WHAT EXACTLY TO MAKE (v1, one exam)

Do **not** build "everything for all exams". Build one product for **CET Senior Secondary**, then duplicate the format.

**Product: "CET Senior Secondary — 45 Day Final Revision Pack" (Hindi)**

| # | File | Spec | Why it's in the box |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rajasthan GK — 120-page notes** | History, art & culture, geography, polity, schemes, districts, fairs, temples, rivers — one topic per 2 pages, bolded keywords, a mind-map at the end of each chapter | This is the core product. Cultural/geography topics repeat every year and generic sellers get them wrong. |
| 2 | **500 one-liners** | A4, 5 columns per page, print-friendly, one fact per line, no design noise | Buyers paste these on walls and revise on the bus. Highest perceived value per rupee. |
| 3 | **"Kya aata hai" — 6-year question analysis** | Topic-wise frequency table: which subject gave how many questions in each exam | Nobody else sells the *pattern*. This is why they trust you. |
| 4 | **3 full mock tests** | 150 questions each, 2 shifts, negative marking, OMR sheet, answer key + explanations | The 2-hour-30-minute habit. This is what turns ₹199 into ₹499. |
| 5 | **45-day plan** | Day-wise: what to read, what to revise, which mock to attempt | Solves the real problem — "kahan se shuru karun". |
| 6 | **Weekly current-affairs sheet (Rajasthan)** | 2 pages, dated, monthly refresh | This is your recurring revenue. Static PDFs get forwarded; updates don't. |
| 7 | **Maths & Reasoning shortcut sheet** | 20 pages, formula + 2 solved examples each | Completes the "don't buy coaching" promise for ₹199 buyers. |

**Format rules (these decide your refund rate):** A4, 12–13 pt Hindi font, dark text on white, every page readable on a ₹8,000 phone screen, every file under 20 MB, one file per subject (never one giant 300 MB blob), and a 3-page free sample with your name watermarked.

**Tools & cost:** Google Docs / MS Word → PDF (free), Canva for the cover and one-liner layouts (free), Google Drive for delivery (free), Razorpay or Topmate for payment (₹0 to start). **Total cash cost: ₹0–₹500. Time: 10–14 evenings.** If you don't have the subject knowledge yourself, pay a B.Ed/university student ₹3,000–₹5,000 for the Rajasthan GK draft — that is a legitimate business expense, not cheating.

## How you make money — the ladder

| Layer | Price | What it is | Realistic take rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₹0 | 10-page sample (Rajasthan GK one-liners) — this is your lead magnet, given in exchange for a WhatsApp number | 30–50% of viewers |
| **Front end** | **₹99** | One-liners pack (500 facts) | the impulse buy |
| **Bump** | **+₹199** | Notes + question-analysis PDF | 30–40% |
| **Upsell** | **₹499** | Everything + 3 mock tests + OMR + 45-day plan | 15–25% |
| **Recurring** | **₹199/month or ₹1,499/year** | Monthly Rajasthan current affairs + new mock tests + doubt WhatsApp group | 5–8% of buyers |

**Why the ₹99 front end and not ₹499 alone:** at ₹99 they buy without thinking, and the pack is small enough that you can deliver it in the time it takes the payment to settle. The profit is in the bump and the yearly pack — a ₹1499 yearly pack that sells 20 times is ₹30,000.

## Getting the first 10 sales this week (no audience, no ads)

**Day 1 — go where they already are.**
- Telegram: search "CET", "Rajasthan CET", "Patwar", "REET", "Rajasthan GK" — join 15–20 groups. **Do not spam.** Post the free sample in groups that allow sharing: *"CET Sen Sec ke liye 10-page Rajasthan GK one-liners bana raha hoon. Free chahiye toh comment karo."*
- Facebook: join Rajasthan government-job groups (they're huge and active).
- WhatsApp: every aspirant you know is in 3–5 groups. Ask one friend to forward the free sample to their group.

**Day 2 — set up the funnel.**
- Create a free Google Form or ManyChat flow: name + WhatsApp number → auto-send the sample PDF.
- Put the paid pack behind a Razorpay/Topmate link: **₹99 one-liners, ₹299 with notes, ₹499 full pack.** Leave the prices visible — hidden prices kill Indian conversion.

**Day 3–7 — post daily, one piece of value per day.**
Reels/Shorts (30–45 s, faceless, screen-record + text):
- *"CET Sen Sec mein Rajasthan GK se kitne questions aate hain? Ye 6 saal ka data dekho."*
- *"Ye 10 GK one-liners pichle exam mein aaye the."*
- *"45 din bache hain. Din 1 se 45 tak ka plan."*

**DM script that converts (use it word-for-word):**
> "Bhai/Behen, sample bhej diya hai. Poora pack ₹99 ka hai — 500 one-liners + pichhle 6 saal ka analysis. Chahiye toh link bhej doon? Payment UPI se ho jayega, PDF turant milega."

**The trick nobody uses:** message **coaching institutes and tuition centres** in Jaipur and nearby towns. They need revision material for their students and will buy a bulk licence (₹2,000–₹5,000 for 50–100 students). That is 3–5 sales in one conversation.

## The ad plan (only after you have 2 organic sales)

Start at **₹300–₹500/day**, Reels placement only, broad targeting (18–32, Rajasthan + Hindi belt), and optimise for conversions once you have 20+ sales. Use the free sample as the ad → landing page → ₹99 pack.
Watch: **CPM > ₹250 or cost per purchase > ₹400 = turn it off.** EdTech CPM in India runs ₹160–₹340, so be patient for the first 3 days.

## Watch-outs (read twice)

- **Copyright will kill you.** Never republish coaching-material PDFs, NCERT scans or paid test-series questions. Write original content, cite public sources, make your own mock questions.
- **Accuracy is the product.** One wrong answer in a mock test and you get a review bomb in the comments. Have a subject teacher verify before you sell, and print "corrections welcome" with a contact.
- **Static PDFs leak on Telegram in days.** That's fine — it's why your money is in the *monthly current-affairs updates, new mocks and the yearly pack*, which forwarding can't replace.
- **Never claim "selection guaranteed"** or imply affiliation with RSSB/REET. You are selling study material, not results.
- **Avoid missing the window.** CET Sen Sec is 1–3 December. Every week of delay costs real money. Ship the ₹99 one-liners within 7 days even if the full pack isn't ready.

**Filming it faceless:** screen-recordings of your own PDFs scrolling on a phone, Hindi AI voiceover or just text-on-screen, and a plain background. No face, no voice needed.

# BRIEF 2 — Indian Wedding Kit 🇮🇳

*(Highest money per sale on this list. Season starts mid-November and runs to February.)*

## The business in one line
Sell editable Canva wedding invitations + WhatsApp video invites + mehendi/haldi decor printables + a ₹ budget tracker, to couples and families planning weddings — anchored against a ₹15,000–₹1,50,000 designer bill.

## Why this works

- Indian wedding content is one of the highest-engagement formats on Indian Instagram, and the moment a family is in wedding-planning mode, **every scroll becomes a purchase decision.**
- The price anchor is absurdly in your favour: ₹499 vs a designer's ₹15,000+, for something the buyer can edit themselves.
- Wedding printables are consistently a top-5 global Etsy digital category (~1.16M monthly views on top listings, average $15.83, single invitation templates at 13,000+ purchases) — and India's version is *bigger*, because we have more weddings, bigger guest lists and a stronger gifting/ritual culture.
- **Timing:** wedding season resumes after Diwali and peaks Nov–Feb. Families finalise invites and decor 3–8 weeks before the date — meaning **right now is exactly when they're buying.**

## WHAT EXACTLY TO MAKE (v1)

| # | File | Spec | Price it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **10 editable invitation suites** | In Canva (share as template link). 5 traditional (royal maroon-gold, jaipuri/peacock, pastel floral, minimal ivory, South-Indian temple border) + 5 modern minimal. Each in **3 formats: WhatsApp digital (1080×1350), A5 print, A4 poster.** Hindi + English text versions. | ₹499 |
| 2 | **5 WhatsApp video invite templates** | CapCut project files, 15–30 seconds, editable name/date/venue, vertical. Styles: royal, floral, minimal, animated-save-the-date, "our story". | ₹999 bundle |
| 3 | **Mehendi / haldi / sangeet decor printables** | Welcome board, signage, favour tags, table numbers, photo-booth props, backdrop lettering, "no-mobile" sign | ₹999 bundle |
| 4 | **Day-of kit** | Seating chart, menu cards (with veg/non-veg marks), baraat + vidai timeline, ritual-day checklist, "who does what" sheet for the family | ₹1,999 full kit |
| 5 | **₹ wedding budget tracker** | Google Sheet with vendor tabs (caterer, decor, photographer, pandit, DJ, mehendi, tent), part-payment tracker, total vs budget, per-head cost calculator | ₹1,999 full kit |
| 6 | **Planning suite** | 12-month checklist, last-30-days plan, bridal beauty timeline, shopping list, hashtag generator | ₹1,999 full kit |
| 7 | **B2B licence** | "Use for client weddings" add-on for decorators/planners | ₹4,999 |

**Make it in Canva (free or Pro ₹499/mo) + CapCut (free).** Time: **two focused weekends.** The Canva template link matters — Indian buyers edit badly, so include a 5-minute Hinglish "how to change the name and colour" video inside the pack, or your refunds will eat your profit.

## The ladder

₹499 invite suite (front) → **+₹299 bump**: decor printable pack → **₹1,999 upsell**: complete kit (invites + video + decor + budget + checklist) → **₹4,999** decorator/planner licence → **₹999** seasonal add-ons later (karwa chauth, naming ceremony, anniversary).

If you sell only one thing, sell the **₹999 combo (invite suite + video invite)** — it's the sweet spot where the price is still impulse and the profit is real: ₹999 × 20 sales = ₹19,980 from a two-weekend build.

## Getting the first 10 sales this week

1. **Local decorators and wedding planners** (the fastest money). Walk into or DM 10 decorators/planners/caterers in Jaipur with: *"Aapke clients ke liye editable invite + decor printables ka pack banaya hai. ₹4,999 mein unlimited client use licence — demo bhej doon?"* One yes = 10 consumer sales.
2. **Instagram:** post the video invite in 5 different styles as a Reel. Caption: *"₹499 mein poora invitation kit — naam, date, colour sab khud change karo."* Ask people to comment "INVITE" for a free single-sample design → DM automation delivers it → your ₹499 link follows. This is the highest-converting funnel in India.
3. **Bridal content pages:** comment thoughtfully on bridal/mehendi pages (not spam) — that's where the buyers are scrolling on their phones at night.
4. **Photographers:** they talk to every couple. Offer a ₹999 affiliate cut per sale they bring.
5. **Pinterest** (yes, in India too): pin "Indian wedding invitation design", "mehendi decor", "haldi decoration" — free, slow, compounds.

**DM script:**
> "Hi! Ye 3 sample invites bhej rahi/raha hoon — aap apna naam aur date change kar sakte ho. ₹499 mein 10 designs + video invite ka pack hai. Aapki date kab hai?"

Asking the wedding date is not small talk — it tells you their urgency and gets them replying.

## Ad plan
₹500/day, Reels only, broad 22–40, targeting Rajasthan + metro India. Creative that works: 12-second screen recording of changing the name and colour on a phone, with the ₹-vs-designer anchor on screen: **"₹499 vs ₹49,000 — same invitation."** Watch cost per purchase: anything above **₹450** means the creative is weak, not the offer.

## Watch-outs
- **Regional correctness matters.** If a Marwari, Jain, Tamil or Sikh family sees a wrong ritual order or a misspelled word, they'll say so in public. Get one person from each community to review the relevant design.
- **Canva literacy.** Half your buyers won't know Canva. The 5-minute setup video is not optional.
- **Seasonality.** Nov–Feb and the summer window (Apr–Jun). During monsoon and Shraddha, sales stop. Build in the off-season, sell in the season.
- **Don't use copyrighted music in the video templates** — give buyers a "add trending audio" step instead, or use royalty-free.
- **AI-generated couple photos** in ads can look fake and hurt trust. Show the actual template, not a fake wedding.

**Filming it faceless:** screen-record your Canva file while you edit the name, then flip through the decor printables on a table with your hands only. No face needed.

# BRIEF 3 — CBSE Kids' Printable Packs 🇮🇳

*(The safest, most repeatable, lowest-stress product on this list.)*

## The business in one line
Sell print-at-home, CBSE/ICSE-aligned worksheet packs per class (UKG–Class 5) to Indian parents and tuition teachers — with a monthly membership that makes it recurring.

## Why this works

- Every Indian parent has the same two problems: **"the child is on the phone" and "holidays are wasted."** A printed worksheet solves both for ₹249.
- Parents **buy repeatedly** — per term, per holiday, per class — and a teacher buys for the whole class.
- **Free-content competition exists** (teachers share PDFs on WhatsApp/Telegram), which is exactly why your product must be *organised*: class-wise, syllabus-aligned, with answer keys and a parent tracker. Free files are random; yours is a system.
- **Zero policy risk.** No health claims, no income claims, no exam-body anything. You can advertise this aggressively with no fear of rejection.
- **Timing:** Jan–Mar is both board-exam season and the cheapest CPM window of the year. Summer holidays (Apr–Jun) are the biggest worksheets season of all — so build now, bank in April.

## WHAT EXACTLY TO MAKE (v1, one class)

Start with **Class 1** (or UKG). One class, done properly, beats six classes done badly.

| # | File | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Maths — 45 pages** | Number sense, addition/subtraction with pictures, shapes, patterns, before/after, greater/less, simple word problems. 8–12 questions per page, big friendly fonts. |
| 2 | **English — 40 pages** | Phonics, CVC words, sight words, reading comprehension (3-line passages), opposites, rhyming, sentence building, cursive practice |
| 3 | **Hindi — 35 pages** | Varnamala, matra practice, shabd-banao, chhote anuched, picture-based writing, vyakaran basics |
| 4 | **EVS / GK — 25 pages** | Body parts, animals, plants, family, festivals, good habits, safety, seasons, India basics |
| 5 | **Brain & activity — 20 pages** | Mazes, spot-the-difference, puzzles, dot-to-dot, colouring, "think and draw" |
| 6 | **Answer key + parent guide** | Every page's answers + "how to use this in 20 minutes a day" + a weekly progress tracker |
| 7 | **Holiday homework pack** | 30 days, one sheet per day, dated, with a "finish and tick" chart — the highest-converting add-on for parents |

**Total: ~180 pages.** Format: A4, black-and-white friendly (parents print at a local shop for ₹1–2/page, and **that matters** — colour pages cost them more, so make it print-cheap and say so).

**Tools:** Canva (free), ChatGPT/Gemini for question ideas and Hindi text, a ₹0–₹500 font/style budget. **Time: about 12–18 hours.** If you know a primary-school teacher, ₹2,000–₹3,000 for a review pass is the best money you'll spend — accuracy on a maths answer key is your reputation.

## The ladder

₹249 per class pack → **+₹199** holiday homework pack bump → **₹999** "all subjects, one class, full year" → **₹1,499/year membership** (fresh sheets monthly + new activity packs) → **₹4,999** school/tuition bulk licence (print for 30 students).

Membership is the real business: 30 parents at ₹1,499/year = ₹44,970/year, recurring, from one class's worth of content. Duplicate per class and it compounds.

## Getting the first 10 sales this week

1. **Your own network first.** You know at least 2 people with children in Class 1–3, or who teach. Give them the pack free for a genuine testimonial + screenshot (with their permission).
2. **School WhatsApp groups.** This is *the* channel. Every parent group has a "sharing" tolerance for genuinely useful material. Approach a teacher or a parent-representative: *"Class 1 ke liye 180-page printable pack banaya hai — CBSE-aligned, answer key ke saath. Group mein share kar sakte hain? Sample free hai."*
3. **Tuition teachers** (the highest-value buyer). They print for many children and buy once, use repeatedly: *"Aapko har mahine naye worksheets chahiye? ₹1,499/year mein monthly naye sheets + answer key."*
4. **Instagram Reels, faceless:** show your own printed pages flipping with a child's hand, text on screen: *"Phone se hatane ka sabse sasta tareeka — ₹249 mein 180 printable pages."* Ask for the comment "WORKSHEET" to get a free 10-page sample.
5. **Pinterest:** pin "worksheets for Class 1", "nursery admission preparation", "holiday homework ideas" — free traffic that compounds for years.

**DM script:**
> "Aapka bachcha kaunsi class mein hai? Main aapko 10-page free sample bhej deta hoon — dekh lijiye, pasand aaye toh poora pack ₹249 ka hai. Print ka kharcha ₹30–₹40 ka hoga."

Telling the parent that printing costs ₹30–₹40 removes the last objection, because their alternative mental price is a ₹400 workbook.

## Ad plan
₹300–₹500/day, Reels, broad 25–45 (parents), India metro + Tier-2. Creative: the *result* — a child quietly writing at a table, then the printed stack, then the price. Watch: cost per purchase **above ₹350** means you're selling the file, not the peace and quiet. Re-cut the hook.

## Watch-outs
- **Mobile readability.** Test every file on a phone. If it doesn't render, you'll get "file open nahi ho raha" DMs and refund requests.
- **Piracy is guaranteed.** Parents forward. That's why your money is in the *membership and the annual pack*, not a single ₹249 file. Never sell one giant PDF; sell class-wise packs with freshness.
- **AI-garbled Hindi.** Check every Hindi worksheet by hand. Garbled matras are instantly visible to a parent and they will roast you in the group.
- **Don't overpromise academically** ("topper ban jayega"). Sell practice and screen-free time — that's what they're buying anyway.
- **Don't ignore November–December and April–June.** Holiday periods are when printables sell hardest; build the "winter break" and "summer holiday" packs in advance.

**Filming it faceless:** overhead shots of printed pages, a child's hand (your own or a relative's, with permission), text-on-screen. Never show children's faces in ads.

# BRIEF 4 — Budget & Cash-Stuffing Systems 🌍

*(Global rank #1 in the full research. Foreign market, USD pricing, $15K/month documented seller.)*

## The business in one line
Sell a "paycheck plan" PDF + auto-calculating spreadsheets + cash-envelope printables to US/UK/Canada/Australia buyers at $12–$29, with a $37 bundle and a $9/month printable club.

## Why this works

- **Documented, named, at scale:** Pretty Arrow (Emily McDermott) reports 30,000+ shop sales; her first spreadsheet hit **$5K/month within 3 months and $15K/month within 6**, and she names Facebook ads as a traffic channel. Top-100 Etsy "Excel budget template" listings average ~3.0M monthly views at ~$5.36 — the category is huge and always-on.
- The aesthetic (pastel binders, envelope stuffing, challenges, cash counting) is *native Reels content*. You don't need to explain the product; you show it.
- **You have an economic edge as an Indian seller:** US Meta CPM is ~9× India's, but your cost of living is not 9× lower — so a $19 average order that breaks even for a US creator is genuinely profitable for you. And this product is 100% faceless, English-text based, and built from a spreadsheet and Canva.

## WHAT EXACTLY TO MAKE (v1)

| # | File | Spec | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Paycheck Plan" — 45-page PDF** | Monthly budget sheet, bill calendar, sinking-funds tracker, debt snowball page, subscription audit, no-spend month tracker, 50/30/20 planner | The $12 front-end product. Clean, minimal, printable. |
| 2 | **12 Google Sheets trackers** | Auto-calculating: monthly budget, expense log, savings goals, debt payoff curve with projection, bill reminder, sinking funds, cash-flow calendar | **This is what makes it worth more than a free printable.** Buyers keep a file they interact with. |
| 3 | **Cash-envelope printables** | A6 envelopes (6 designs), 100-envelope challenge, 52-week challenge, £/$/€ versions, tracker cards | The viral TikTok/Reels format. People film themselves using this. |
| 4 | **"Binder kit"** | Cover page, dividers, tabs, category labels, "sinking funds" binder pages | Justifies the $19 order bump. |
| 5 | **5-minute setup video** | Screen recording: how to use the sheet, how to set it up in 5 minutes | Removes the #1 refund reason ("I don't understand sheets"). |

**Tools:** Google Sheets (free), Canva (free/Pro), Loom or your phone's screen recorder. **Time: one weekend** — the Sheets are the only part that takes thinking, and they're 80% of the value.

**Currency versions matter:** build $ first (US), then duplicate into £ (UK), € (EU) and A$ (AU). Same product, ×4 markets, one build.

## The ladder

**$12** Paycheck Plan → **+$9** binder + envelopes bump → **$37** "Pay off debt vault" (everything + debt-payoff video + templates) → **$9/month** printable club (new challenge each month) → **$47** year membership.

At $12 front + 30% take on the $9 bump + 15% take on $37, your average order is roughly **$23**. US cost per purchase is $25–$70 — so the front end alone rarely pays; **the ladder and the back end are the business.** This is not optional thinking; it's the reason this idea is ranked #1 globally.

## Getting the first 20 sales (this one is different — you're buying attention, not finding neighbours)

1. **Etsy first, ads second.** List 3–5 variations (budget planner, cash stuffing kit, sinking funds, debt payoff, ADHD budget) with keyword-optimised titles. Etsy search brings buyers *already searching* — no ad spend, no audience needed.
2. **Pinterest.** This category is a Pinterest natural: pin every page mockup as its own pin ("sinking funds tracker", "cash envelope system", "debt snowball printable"). Free, slow, compounding — and the cheapest traffic you will ever get.
3. **Facebook groups:** "Dave Ramsey", "debt free community", "cash stuffing", "budgeting" groups. Give value, never spam links, and answer questions with real help. Then share when asked.
4. **Reels/TikTok (faceless):** hands-only ASMR-style cash stuffing, "print with me" videos, "£3 vs £80" style anchors. Add captions and a "link in bio" CTA.
5. **Meta ads to US/UK/AU once Etsy shows you a converting listing.** Hold off on ads until you have organic proof first — global CPMs are ~9× India's, so mistakes cost 9× more.

**Where you sell & get paid:** Gumroad or Payhip (fastest, no monthly fee — check current payout support for India and prefer PayPal/bank transfer), or Shopify + Stripe if you want bumps and upsells properly. Etsy for search demand. Price in USD, not ₹.

## Ad plan (once you're ready)
$10–$20/day, US/UK/AU, Reels + Feed, "Purchase" optimisation after 20+ sales. Because nothing here is regulated, you can test aggressively. Target a **cost per purchase under $25**; if it's above $45, the creative is the problem.

## Watch-outs
- **Never touch financial advice.** You are selling a printable tracker, not advice, not returns, not "get rich". No income claims in ads — Meta rejects "make money" framing, and US financial-claim scrutiny is real.
- **Never say "broke", "bad with money", "you're struggling".** That trips Meta's personal-attributes rule and hurts trust. Sell control, calm and the ritual.
- **Don't overprice early.** $12 is the right front end. The money is the ladder.
- **Don't build in ₹.** US buyers don't buy ₹-priced printables at scale; use USD and a US-friendly checkout.
- **Q4 CPMs spike 20–40%** (Black Friday week can be 2–3× normal). Test in Jan–Mar, scale Sep–Nov.

**Filming it faceless:** clear/print/flip shots of the sheets, hands stuffing envelopes, screen recordings of the Google Sheet auto-calculating. Zero face, zero voiceover needed — captions do the work.

# BRIEF 5 — ADHD / Neurodivergent Planners 🌍

*(Global rank #2 in the research, and the highest documented single-product sales of anything on this list.)*

## The business in one line
Sell a low-stimulus, undated planner + executive-function workbook to the huge (and underserved) US/UK audience that has "tried every planner" — at $17–$29, with a $49 system bundle and a $12/month community.

## Why this works

- **The proof is unusually strong:** a single ADHD digital planner reports **50,000+ sales**; on Gumroad a top ADHD planner shows **3,100+ sales at $17 (~$52K)** plus a **$99 version with 225 sales (~$22K)** — two products, ~$74K. Trend trackers list ADHD tools as a rising category with simpler layouts outperforming busy ones.
- The buyer is **identity-driven and emotionally motivated**. They're not comparing specs — they're saying "finally, something built for my brain."
- It's a **PDF and a notebook-style design**. No technical build, no inventory, no regulation, and completely faceless to run.
- **You can charge much more than most digital products** because the pain is chronic and the alternative is a $200 coach or a $60 planner that failed.

## WHAT EXACTLY TO MAKE (v1)

| # | File | Spec | Why it converts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Undated low-stimulus planner — 60 pages** | Daily + weekly spreads with **3 boxes, not 30**. No colour noise, no "life goals", no motivational quotes. Big white space. Undated so it sells forever. | Low-stimulus design is the whole point — busy planners are what failed them before. |
| 2 | **Brain-dump + task-sorter** | "Everything in my head" page → then a "3 things today" page with a suggested sorting rule | Solves the actual daily failure: too many open loops. |
| 3 | **Executive-function workbook — 25 pages** | Starting tasks, task initiation/transition alarms, time-blindness clocks, money friction, "waiting mode", doom-box management | This is the "$24 bump" — it's the reason a $17 buyer upgrades. |
| 4 | **Dopamine menu poster** | A one-page printable list of quick resets (5-min → 15-min → 1-hour) | Extremely shareable; drives organic reach on its own. |
| 5 | **Body-doubling audio — 10 minutes** | Your voice or an AI voice: "we're starting together, I'll check in at 5 and 10" | Very low competition, very high perceived value, zero marginal cost. |
| 6 | **GoodNotes + printable versions** | Same planner as hyperlinked PDF for tablet users | Doubles the addressable audience for zero extra design work. |
| 7 | **Budget page (optional)** | The most requested add-on in ADHD communities | Clean upsell into "money + focus" bundle later. |

**Tools:** Canva (free/Pro), Google Docs, Audacity or CapCut for audio. **Time: 1–2 weekends.** Do **not** make it beautiful — make it *calm*. That's the design brief.

## The ladder

**$17** planner → **+$7** teacher/student or money add-on bump → **$27** planner + executive-function workbook → **$49** "complete focus system" (everything + audio + Notion dashboard) → **$12/month** body-doubling + accountability community.

Average order at $17 front end with a 30% take on a $7 bump and 15% on $27 is roughly **$23–$26**. US cost per purchase averages $25–$70, which means: **the $49 bundle and the $12/month community are what make this profitable**, not the planner alone. Build them on day one, even if the community is just a private WhatsApp/Discord with a weekly check-in post.

## Getting the first 20 sales

1. **Etsy (start here).** Title for search: "ADHD Planner Printable | Undated Low-Stimulus Daily Planner | Executive Function Workbook | Instant Download". Etsy buyers are *already looking* — this is the single fastest first-sale channel for this product.
2. **Pinterest.** Pin every page individually ("ADHD planner", "brain dump template", "body doubling"). This category lives on Pinterest.
3. **Communities (with care).** Reddit (r/ADHD — read the rules, participate genuinely, don't drop links), ADHD Facebook groups, Discord servers. Share the free Dopamine Menu poster as a gift. The buyers come to you.
4. **TikTok / Reels, faceless:** page flips with a text hook. This is the highest-performing creative format in this niche.
5. **Then Meta ads** to US/UK 18+, once Etsy proves the listing converts.

## Ad plan (once you're ready)
$10–$20/day, US/UK, 18+ only, Reels. Highest-performing hooks are *identity* hooks, not feature hooks. Target cost per purchase under $30; above $50, change the hook, not the offer.

## Watch-outs (this is the most sensitive category on the list)

- **Never make medical claims.** No "treats", "cures", "diagnose", "ADHD management system". Sell *tools and structure*, never treatment.
- **18+ targeting** on anything referencing ADHD, and avoid "do you struggle to focus?" second-person framing — that trips Meta's personal-attributes rule. Say "a low-stimulus planner for people who've tried every planner" instead.
- **Don't fake a diagnosis story.** The audience is highly sensitive to inauthenticity and will call it out. Frame yourself as the builder of the tool, not a patient.
- **Refunds spike if it's not immediately usable.** Include a one-page "start here in 5 minutes" guide, or your refund rate will quietly eat 10–15% of revenue.
- **The category is being copied right now.** Your moat is the workbook, the audio and the community — the three things lazy sellers won't build.

**Filming it faceless:** slow page flips on a wooden desk, hands-only, text-on-screen hooks, no voice. This is the most faceless-friendly product of the five.

## The five mistakes that kill all five of these

1. **Building before selling.** Ship the ₹99 version in 7 days, sell it, *then* build the ₹499 pack with the money.
